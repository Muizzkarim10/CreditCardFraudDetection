Although the roc-auc is 98% the recall for fraud class is 58% meaning
we are on;y catching 58 out of every 100 fraud transactions, with a Precision
of 87%, when the model says the transactions is a fraud it right 87% of the time.

The model Accuracy is 100%, but in this case due to highly imbalance data the model
can simply have 99% just by predicting only legitimate transactions, which is why we
get 100% accuracy even with 40 fraudulent cases missed.
